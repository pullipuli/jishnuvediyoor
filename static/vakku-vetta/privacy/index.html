Privacy Policy

വാക്ക് വേട്ട (Vakku Vetta) · Last updated 23 October 2026

Vakku Vetta is a Malayalam word-search game for children and students, available as a web game and as an app for Android and iOS.

We do not collect any personal information.

  • The game does not ask for names, email addresses, photos, location or any other personal details. A player can type a nickname for the leaderboard; it stays on their own device.
  • There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no third-party tracking.
  • The Android and iOS apps do not connect to the internet. They work fully offline.
  • Game progress (stars, leaderboard scores and sound on/off) is stored only on the device. It is never sent anywhere, and it is deleted when the app is uninstalled or the browser's site data is cleared.
  • The apps contain no links to websites, social media or other apps.

Because no data is collected, no data is shared with anyone.
